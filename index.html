My Story

I've always been driven to solve problems using data — leveraging spreadsheets, analytics, and structured systems to improve operations and reduce costs.

Growing up, I was deeply hooked on video games and computer games. What started as curiosity about games eventually turned into a deeper fascination with computers, systems, and how applications work behind the scenes. That early interest naturally evolved into exploring spreadsheets, analytics tools, automation, and problem-solving through technology.

Over the years, I built analytics systems for real-world use — from volunteer registration tracking and workflow dashboards to managing over 50 community programs, coordinating hundreds of volunteers, and delivering measurable impact.

What consistently frustrated me was seeing organizations make decisions without clear data — especially when simple systems could reveal inefficiencies and opportunities for improvement.

When fuel prices started rising, I experienced this problem firsthand. I wanted a clear way to understand and control my own travel costs, but there was no simple, practical tool available. So I built one — a fuel cost calculator.

That's when I realized this wasn't just a personal challenge. Many individuals and businesses were facing rising operational costs without any structured way to analyze or reduce them.

I saw a gap: while data is everywhere, practical systems that turn data into decisions are rare.

Today, I build simple, data-driven tools and analytics systems that help organizations reduce costs, improve efficiency, and make confident decisions — whether in business operations or community impact.
